## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **AI Gaming Platform** ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to safeguarding user privacy and transparency while providing an advanced gaming telemetry, studio intelligence, live ops, and procedural dev tools platform.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you access and use the AI Gaming Platform mobile application.

---

## 2. Summary of Key Principles

- **Zero Mandatory Personal Information:** We do not require you to provide personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, physical address, email address, or phone number to use the Application.
- **Client-Side Data Storage:** All studio telemetry, game configurations, live-event setups, and market analytics are stored locally on your device via standard browser and webview storage mechanisms (e.g., `localStorage`).
- **No Sensitive Permissions:** The Application does not request or require access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise GPS location, biometric data, photos, or file system outside of application-specific sandboxes.
- **Offline Functionality:** Core telemetry simulations and game development calculators operate seamlessly without an active internet connection.

---

## 3. Information We Collect and Process

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
When you interact with the Application—such as toggling analytics tabs, configuring AI game dev tools, reviewing live events, or examining market projections—these settings and simulation states are preserved locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to our external servers.

### B. Device and Technical Information (Standard Diagnostics)
If the Application integrates standard third-party developer libraries (such as Google Play Services or optional crash reporting), temporary technical diagnostic data may be processed automatically:
- Operating system version and device model
- Crash reports, performance benchmarks, and battery diagnostics
- Standard non-personalized diagnostic identifiers

### C. Information We Do NOT Collect
- Personal contact details or user accounts
- Payment credentials or financial bank accounts (all revenue figures in the app represent simulated analytics)
- Precise geolocation data
- Audio, video, or photo recordings

---

## 4. How We Use Information

Information processed within the Application is strictly utilized to:
1. Provide responsive rendering of gaming studio telemetry and real-time charts.
2. Remember user preferences across sessions (such as selected tabs and tool settings).
3. Ensure platform stability, resolve unexpected app terminations, and optimize performance.

## 5. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The Application may display non-intrusive advertisements or utilize trusted third-party analytics SDKs provided by platforms such as Google (Google AdMob, Firebase). These providers may collect anonymous, non-personal advertising identifiers governed by their respective privacy policies:
- **Google Play Services:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google AdMob:** [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

Users may reset or restrict ad tracking at any time via their Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads > Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**).

---

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because studio configurations and simulation metrics reside locally on your device:
- You retain complete ownership and control over your stored data.
- You can instantly delete all Application data by clearing the app cache/storage in your device settings (**Settings > Apps > AI Gaming Platform > Storage > Clear Data**) or by uninstalling the Application.
